A reading project Stanisław Lem · 1986

Fiasco

Essays, visualizations, and creative responses to Lem's last novel — a first-contact mission in which each step was reasonable on its own terms, and irreversible in combination.

SignalFire

In Fiasco, the spacecraft Hermes crosses interstellar space to greet the first alien civilization humanity has ever found — and everything the crew does to open a conversation makes war more likely. This site examines how that happens, one decision at a time, and why a novel from 1986 keeps describing the present.
