In Fiasco*, the spacecraft Hermes crosses interstellar space to greet the first alien civilization humanity has ever found — and everything the crew does to open a conversation makes war more likely. This site examines how that happens, one decision at a time.

* Fiasco is a science fiction novel by Polish author Stanisław Lem, first published in 1986. The book was translated into English by Michael Kandel in 1987.

All names, events, and terminology on this site are drawn from the Kandel English translation. The timelines, thematic notes, glossary, and visualizations here are original commentary and analysis — a transformative work of criticism, not a substitute for the novel. Fiasco remains the copyright of the Estate of Stanisław Lem and its publishers; readers are encouraged to seek out the book itself.

This site exists to support readers of Fiasco as a companion to keep alongside the book rather than a shortcut around it.
